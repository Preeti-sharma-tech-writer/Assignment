Warning

The access_token issued by Tarinsash is valid for 30 days. The advertiser or managing agency must manually refresh the token every 30 days and update it in the General settings section, as required by Tarinsash.

If the token expires, postbacks to Tarinsash will fail until you enter a new token. There is no workaround.

Agency-managed traffic: If an agency promotes your traffic, the agency must also enter and maintain the token in its integration settings.

Review or update the token in AppsFlyer.

Set a 30-day calendar reminder. Set the first reminder for 30 days after the token was issued, and repeat it every 30 days.

If you're not sure how to obtain or refresh the token, contact your Tarinsash account manager.
